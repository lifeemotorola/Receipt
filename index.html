Official cover page

A full-page or receipt-piece cover with the school name, address and the receipt-number range — every book looks official.

Original & carbon copies

Each receipt number prints as an Original sheet plus a highlighted carbon-copy sheet, exactly like a real carbon receipt book.

Smart numbering

Sequential across the book, with the option of triplicate mode (the same number on all three slips of a sheet).

Receipt table ready to use

Registration, semesters, uniform, WAEC, field trip, total and balance — edit the rows and columns to match your fee list.

Cut lines & print-ready

Dashed or solid cut guides between slips and under each sheet, sized for US-Letter or A4 landscape paper.

Settings remembered

Your school details and customizations are saved in the browser, so next time the book opens exactly as you left it.
